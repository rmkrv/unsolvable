# Repository working rules

## Local product and design notes

- Keep private, informal, or temporary product-owner feedback in `docs/LOCAL_*.md` files.
- Files matching `docs/LOCAL_*.md` are checkout-local and must remain ignored by Git. Do not stage or commit them.
- Keep durable architecture, setup, and contributor documentation in tracked Markdown files such as `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `README.md`, and `docs/PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`.
- When temporary feedback becomes an approved decision, rewrite the decision in the appropriate tracked document rather than committing the local note verbatim.
